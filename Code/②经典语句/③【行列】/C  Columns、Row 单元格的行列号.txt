 
Sub 当前单元格的行列号()
    MsgBox ActiveCell.Row, , "当前单元格行号："
    MsgBox ActiveCell.EntireRow.Row, , "当前单元格行号："
    
    MsgBox ActiveCell.Column, , "当前单元格列号："
    MsgBox ActiveCell.EntireColumn.Column, , "当前单元格列号："
    
    MsgBox Split(ActiveCell.EntireColumn.Address(0, 0), ":")(0), , "读取当前单元格列字母：即列标"
End Sub

'Cells(1, "C")即C1单元格
 MsgBox Cells(1, "C").EntireRow.Row 
 MsgBox Cells(1, "C").EntireColumn.Column '字母C可以通过 下拉列表框ComboBox获取
